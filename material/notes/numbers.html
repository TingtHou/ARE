Numbers I could not verify — read these in your own code

Do not memorise anything for these until you check them
  • IBC Table 602 — exterior wall fire-resistance rating by fire separation distance and occupancy. Not retrieved.
  • IBC Table 705.8 — maximum area of exterior wall openings by fire separation distance and protection. Not retrieved.
  • IBC Table 716.1(2) and 716.1(3) — fire door and fire window assembly ratings. Not retrieved. This is the most consequential gap on this page: it is the table behind every "what rating does this door need" item. The pattern in your Panic Notes is right for 4-hour and 2-hour walls and wrong for 1-hour fire barriers and corridors — see the PPD page.
  • IBC Table 506.3.3 — the 2021 tabular frontage increase values that replaced the 2015/2018 formula.
  • IBC §504.2 — the specific sprinkler height and storey increase values.
  • IBC Table 508.4 — required separation of occupancies, and the §509 incidental use table beyond the rows on the PDD page.
  • IBC Table 601 Types IV-A, IV-B and IV-C columns — the mass timber ratings are from a trade summary, not code text.
  • Full ACI 318 Tables 8.3.1.1 and 9.3.1.1 — only the interior-panel two-way coefficient and the one-way slab table were confirmed. Beam minimum thicknesses were not.
  • ASCE 7 stage floor and mechanical room live loads — sources conflicted (100 vs 150 psf for stages) and no clean value was found for mechanical rooms.
  • Typical dead loads per assembly in psf — no reusable authoritative table found. ASCE 7 Chapter 3 and its commentary are the right source.
  • General conditions, GC overhead and profit, subcontractor markup and bond percentages — widely published but not confirmable to a professional body.
  • LEED v4.1 per-category point weightings — usgbc.org blocked automated access.
  • Precast double-T span and depth data — PCI tables not retrieved.
  • Sound velocity by material — two of your own sources disagree and MEEB has no table to arbitrate.
  • DOAS plenum-depth saving versus a conventional VAV system — no citable figure found.
