## Practice Management (PcM) and Project Management (PjM)

- The PcM and PjM notes pages, the PcM/PjM flashcards and the weeks 15–22 plan come from the *PcM + PjM Command Deck*. It was compiled from the NCARB ARE 5.0 specifications, AIA B101–2017, B101–2007 and A101–2007, and *The Architecture Student’s Handbook of Professional Practice*.
- The PcM practice questions (q157–q212) are from **arequestions.com** (Elif Bayram, ARE 5.0 Practice Questions), as collected in the owner's quiz file. They remain the property of their author and are included for the owner's own study. Questions whose text was truncated or incomplete in the source were left out.
- The PjM questions q213–q244 and the PcM questions q245–q251 were written for this site, in the bank's style, from AIA B101–2017, A201–2017 and C401–2017 and *The Architect's Handbook of Professional Practice*.
